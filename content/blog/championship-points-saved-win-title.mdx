    <p><strong>Definition:</strong> A <em>championship point</em> is a match point that, if won by the leading player, immediately decides the title — in other words, the opponent is one point away from losing the championship.</p>
    <p className="mt-2"><strong>Why it matters:</strong> Saving one or more championship points in a final and then winning the match is rare and highly significant; it often changes the historical narrative of the event and is tracked separately in match records.</p>
    <ul className="list-disc ml-5 mt-3">
      <li>Context-specific: it is a match point that occurs in a final (decides the championship).</li>
      <li>Statistical interest: analysts count how many championship points were saved or converted in finals.</li>
    </ul>
    <p className="mt-3 text-xs text-gray-400">Example: if Player A leads 5–4, 40–30 in the deciding set of a final, Player A has a championship point.</p>
