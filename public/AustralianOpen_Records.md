## [Titles won](/tournaments/australian-open/records/count/titles)

The Australian Open men's singles title record belongs to [🇷🇸 Novak Djokovic](/players/novak-djokovic/matches), who has won the trophy <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">10 times</span> ([2008](/tournaments/australian-open/2008), [2011](/tournaments/australian-open/2011)–[2013](/tournaments/australian-open/2013), [2015](/tournaments/australian-open/2015)–[2016](/tournaments/australian-open/2016), [2019](/tournaments/australian-open/2019)–[2021](/tournaments/australian-open/2021), and [2023](/tournaments/australian-open/2023)). [🇨🇭 Roger Federer](/players/roger-federer/matches) follows with <span class="mc-emerald mc-bold">6 titles</span>, ahead of [🇺🇸 Andre Agassi](/players/andre-agassi/matches) (<span class="mc-blue">4</span>) and [🇸🇪 Mats Wilander](/players/mats-wilander/matches) (<span class="mc-blue">3</span>). Two-time champions include [🇪🇸 Rafael Nadal](/players/rafael-nadal/matches), [🇮🇹 Jannik Sinner](/players/jannik-sinner/matches), [🇩🇪 Boris Becker](/players/boris-becker/matches), [🇺🇸 Pete Sampras](/players/pete-sampras/matches), and [🇦🇺 Ken Rosewall](/players/ken-rosewall/matches). The [2026](/tournaments/australian-open/2026) title went to [🇪🇸 Carlos Alcaraz](/players/carlos-alcaraz/matches), who defeated Djokovic in the final. → [Full titles leaderboard](/tournaments/australian-open/records/count/titles)

## [Winning streaks & Consecutive titles](/tournaments/australian-open/records/streak)

[🇷🇸 Novak Djokovic](/players/novak-djokovic/matches) owns the tournament's longest winning streak with <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">33 consecutive victories</span>, ahead of [🇺🇸 Andre Agassi](/players/andre-agassi/matches) (<span class="mc-emerald mc-bold">26</span>) and [🇨🇿 Ivan Lendl](/players/ivan-lendl/matches) (<span class="mc-blue">20</span>). Djokovic has twice won <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">three consecutive titles</span>: [2011](/tournaments/australian-open/2011)–[2013](/tournaments/australian-open/2013) and [2019](/tournaments/australian-open/2019)–[2021](/tournaments/australian-open/2021). [🇮🇹 Jannik Sinner](/players/jannik-sinner/matches) won back-to-back titles in [2024](/tournaments/australian-open/2024) and [2025](/tournaments/australian-open/2025). → [Full streak leaderboard](/tournaments/australian-open/records/streak)

## Titles without dropping a set

[🇨🇭 Roger Federer](/players/roger-federer/matches) won the [2007](/tournaments/australian-open/2007) Australian Open without losing a set across <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">seven matches</span>. [🇦🇺 Ken Rosewall](/players/ken-rosewall/matches) also completed the [1971](/tournaments/australian-open/1971) title run without dropping a set, although the championship path then involved fewer matches. These performances illustrate why set-loss records must be viewed in the context of the draw and tournament format.

## [Age records](/tournaments/australian-open/records/ages/main)

The [youngest champion](/tournaments/australian-open/records/ages/titles) in the recorded Open Era is [🇸🇪 Mats Wilander](/players/mats-wilander/matches), who began his [1983](/tournaments/australian-open/1983) title campaign aged approximately <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">19 years and 98 days</span>. The oldest is [🇦🇺 Ken Rosewall](/players/ken-rosewall/matches), aged approximately <span class="mc-orange mc-bold">37 years and 55 days</span> at the start of the [1972 edition](/tournaments/australian-open/1972), which began in December 1971. [🇨🇭 Roger Federer](/players/roger-federer/matches) won his last Melbourne title in [2018](/tournaments/australian-open/2018) aged about <span class="mc-orange">36 years and 160 days</span> at tournament start. → [Youngest & oldest champions](/tournaments/australian-open/records/ages/titles) · [Age records by round](/tournaments/australian-open/records/ages/youngestrounds)
