## [Titles won](/tournaments/indian-wells-masters/records/count/titles)

[🇨🇭 Roger Federer](/players/roger-federer/matches) and [🇷🇸 Novak Djokovic](/players/novak-djokovic/matches) share the all-time tournament title record with <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">5 championships each</span>. Federer won in [2004](/tournaments/indian-wells-masters/2004)–[2006](/tournaments/indian-wells-masters/2006), [2012](/tournaments/indian-wells-masters/2012) and [2017](/tournaments/indian-wells-masters/2017); Djokovic won in [2008](/tournaments/indian-wells-masters/2008), [2011](/tournaments/indian-wells-masters/2011) and [2014](/tournaments/indian-wells-masters/2014)–[2016](/tournaments/indian-wells-masters/2016). [🇺🇸 Jimmy Connors](/players/jimmy-connors/matches), [🇺🇸 Michael Chang](/players/michael-chang/matches) and [🇪🇸 Rafael Nadal](/players/rafael-nadal/matches) have <span class="mc-emerald mc-bold">3 titles</span> apiece. Two-time winners include [🇪🇸 Carlos Alcaraz](/players/carlos-alcaraz/matches), [🇩🇪 Boris Becker](/players/boris-becker/matches), [🇺🇸 Jim Courier](/players/jim-courier/matches), [🇺🇸 Pete Sampras](/players/pete-sampras/matches), [🇦🇺 Lleyton Hewitt](/players/lleyton-hewitt/matches), and [🇺🇸 Roscoe Tanner](/players/roscoe-tanner/matches). ## Titles without dropping a set

[🇮🇹 Jannik Sinner](/players/jannik-sinner/matches) won the [2026](/tournaments/indian-wells-masters/2026) title without dropping a set across <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">six matches</span>, culminating in a straight-sets victory over [🇷🇺 Daniil Medvedev](/players/daniil-medvedev/matches). Earlier straight-set title campaigns in the recorded results include [🇨🇭 Roger Federer](/players/roger-federer/matches) in [2005](/tournaments/indian-wells-masters/2005) and [🇪🇸 Rafael Nadal](/players/rafael-nadal/matches) in [2007](/tournaments/indian-wells-masters/2007). These records should be interpreted in light of the number of matches in each edition.
