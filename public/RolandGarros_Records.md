## [Titles won](/tournaments/roland-garros/records/count/titles)

The all-time record for titles at Roland Garros belongs to [🇪🇸 Rafael Nadal](/players/rafael-nadal/matches), who has lifted the Coupe des Mousquetaires <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">14 times</span> — a mark that looks virtually unassailable. Behind him, on <span class="mc-emerald mc-bold">6 titles</span>, stands [🇸🇪 Bjorn Borg](/players/bjorn-borg/matches). Others to have won the tournament more than once: [🇸🇪 Mats Wilander](/players/mats-wilander/matches) <span class="mc-blue">(3)</span>, [🇨🇿 Ivan Lendl](/players/ivan-lendl/matches) <span class="mc-blue">(3)</span>, [🇧🇷 Gustavo Kuerten](/players/gustavo-kuerten/matches) <span class="mc-blue">(3)</span>, [🇷🇸 Novak Djokovic](/players/novak-djokovic/matches) <span class="mc-blue">(3)</span>, [🇨🇿 Jan Kodes](/players/jan-kodes/matches) <span class="mc-blue">(2)</span>, [🇺🇸 Jim Courier](/players/jim-courier/matches) <span class="mc-blue">(2)</span>, [🇪🇸 Sergi Bruguera](/players/sergi-bruguera/matches) <span class="mc-blue">(2)</span>, [🇪🇸 Carlos Alcaraz](/players/carlos-alcaraz/matches) <span class="mc-blue">(2)</span>.

## [Winning streaks & Consecutive titles](/tournaments/roland-garros/records/streak)

[🇪🇸 Rafael Nadal](/players/rafael-nadal/matches) holds the record for the longest unbeaten run: <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">39 consecutive wins</span> between [2010](/tournaments/roland-garros/2010) and [2015](/tournaments/roland-garros/2015). He also holds the record for consecutive titles: <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">5 in a row</span> from [2010](/tournaments/roland-garros/2010) to [2014](/tournaments/roland-garros/2014). Second on the streak list: [🇸🇪 Bjorn Borg](/players/bjorn-borg/matches) (<span class="mc-emerald">28</span>), followed by [🇺🇸 Jim Courier](/players/jim-courier/matches) (<span class="mc-blue">20</span>), [🇪🇸 Sergi Bruguera](/players/sergi-bruguera/matches) (<span class="mc-blue">19</span>), [🇨🇿 Ivan Lendl](/players/ivan-lendl/matches) (<span class="mc-blue">18</span>), [🇧🇷 Gustavo Kuerten](/players/gustavo-kuerten/matches) (<span class="mc-blue">17</span>). → [Full streak leaderboard](/tournaments/roland-garros/records/streak)

## [Age records](/tournaments/roland-garros/records/ages/main)

The [youngest champion](/tournaments/roland-garros/records/ages/titles) in tournament history is [🇺🇸 Michael Chang](/players/michael-chang/matches), who won the title at just <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">17 years and 96 days</span> in [1989](/tournaments/roland-garros/1989), defeating [🇸🇪 Stefan Edberg](/players/stefan-edberg/matches) in the final. The oldest champion is [🇷🇸 Novak Djokovic](/players/novak-djokovic/matches) at <span class="mc-orange mc-bold">36 years and 6 days</span> ([2023](/tournaments/roland-garros/2023)).

The [youngest player to win a match](/tournaments/roland-garros/records/ages/youngestrounds) is [🇺🇸 Michael Chang](/players/michael-chang/matches) at <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">16 years and 90 days</span> ([1988](/tournaments/roland-garros/1988)), while the [oldest match winner](/tournaments/roland-garros/records/ages/oldestrounds) is [🇦🇺 Bob Howe](/players/bob-howe/matches) at <span class="mc-orange mc-bold">44 years and 295 days</span> ([1970](/tournaments/roland-garros/1970)). The youngest participant ever is [🇫🇷 Francois Errard](/players/francois-errard/matches), who took to the court at <span class="mc-gold">15 years and 255 days</span> in [1983](/tournaments/roland-garros/1983).

## [Timespan records](/tournaments/roland-garros/records/timespan)

[🇪🇸 Rafael Nadal](/players/rafael-nadal/matches) holds the record for the longest span between first and last title: <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">17 years</span> ([2005](/tournaments/roland-garros/2005)–[2022](/tournaments/roland-garros/2022)). The longest gap between two title wins by the same player is <span class="mc-emerald mc-bold">5 years</span>, achieved by [🇷🇸 Novak Djokovic](/players/novak-djokovic/matches) between [2016](/tournaments/roland-garros/2016) and [2021](/tournaments/roland-garros/2021). The tournament has been held from [1968](/tournaments/roland-garros/1968) ([🇦🇺 Ken Rosewall](/players/ken-rosewall/matches)) through [2025](/tournaments/roland-garros/2025) ([🇪🇸 Carlos Alcaraz](/players/carlos-alcaraz/matches)). → [Full timespan records](/tournaments/roland-garros/records/timespan)

## [Ten youngest champions in history](/tournaments/roland-garros/records/ages/titles)

Second is [🇸🇪 Mats Wilander](/players/mats-wilander/matches) at <span class="mc-blue">17 years and 274 days</span> ([1982](/tournaments/roland-garros/1982)), followed by [🇸🇪 Bjorn Borg](/players/bjorn-borg/matches) (<span class="mc-blue">17 years and 361 days</span>, [1974](/tournaments/roland-garros/1974)), [🇪🇸 Rafael Nadal](/players/rafael-nadal/matches) (<span class="mc-blue">18 years and 354 days</span>, [2005](/tournaments/roland-garros/2005)), and [🇸🇪 Bjorn Borg](/players/bjorn-borg/matches) (<span class="mc-blue">18 years and 362 days</span>, [1975](/tournaments/roland-garros/1975)).

The rest of the top ten: [🇪🇸 Rafael Nadal](/players/rafael-nadal/matches) (<span class="mc-blue">19 years and 360 days</span>, [2006](/tournaments/roland-garros/2006)), [🇧🇷 Gustavo Kuerten](/players/gustavo-kuerten/matches) (<span class="mc-blue">20 years and 257 days</span>, [1997](/tournaments/roland-garros/1997)), [🇸🇪 Mats Wilander](/players/mats-wilander/matches) (<span class="mc-blue">20 years and 277 days</span>, [1985](/tournaments/roland-garros/1985)), [🇺🇸 Jim Courier](/players/jim-courier/matches) (<span class="mc-blue">20 years and 283 days</span>, [1991](/tournaments/roland-garros/1991)), and [🇪🇸 Rafael Nadal](/players/rafael-nadal/matches) (<span class="mc-blue">20 years and 359 days</span>, [2007](/tournaments/roland-garros/2007)).
