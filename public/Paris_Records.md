The Rolex Paris Masters has been a fixture of the indoor tennis calendar since **1986**. The records below cover completed men's singles editions through **2025**, while the **2026 tournament is in progress**. The statistics and linked leaderboards follow TennisMyLife's Open Era tournament database.

## [Titles won](/tournaments/paris-masters/records/count/titles)

[🇷🇸 Novak Djokovic](/players/novak-djokovic/matches) leads the Paris Masters champions with <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">7 titles</span>, claimed in [2009](/tournaments/paris-masters/2009), [2013](/tournaments/paris-masters/2013), [2014](/tournaments/paris-masters/2014), [2015](/tournaments/paris-masters/2015), [2019](/tournaments/paris-masters/2019), [2021](/tournaments/paris-masters/2021), and [2023](/tournaments/paris-masters/2023). [🇩🇪 Boris Becker](/players/boris-becker/matches) and [🇷🇺 Marat Safin](/players/marat-safin/matches) share second place on <span class="mc-emerald mc-bold">3 titles</span> each. [🇺🇸 Andre Agassi](/players/andre-agassi/matches) and [🇺🇸 Pete Sampras](/players/pete-sampras/matches) each won <span class="mc-blue mc-bold">2</span>. The most recent completed champion is [🇮🇹 Jannik Sinner](/players/jannik-sinner/matches), who defeated [🇨🇦 Felix Auger-Aliassime](/players/felix-auger-aliassime/matches) in the [2025 final](/tournaments/paris-masters/2025). → [Full titles leaderboard](/tournaments/paris-masters/records/count/titles)

## [Wins & Matches played](/tournaments/paris-masters/records/count/wins)

Djokovic also holds the record for the most main-draw match wins at Paris, with <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">50 victories</span> in the tournament's records tables. Becker follows with <span class="mc-emerald mc-bold">29</span>, ahead of [🇨🇿 Tomas Berdych](/players/tomas-berdych/matches) on <span class="mc-blue">27</span> and [🇧🇬 Grigor Dimitrov](/players/grigor-dimitrov/matches) on <span class="mc-blue">25</span>. The matches-played record belongs to Djokovic as well, at <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">59</span>, followed by Berdych (<span class="mc-emerald">38</span>), Dimitrov (<span class="mc-blue">37</span>), and Becker (<span class="mc-blue">36</span>). → [Full wins leaderboard](/tournaments/paris-masters/records/count/wins) · [Most matches played](/tournaments/paris-masters/records/count/played)

## [Winning streaks & Consecutive titles](/tournaments/paris-masters/records/streak)

[🇷🇸 Novak Djokovic](/players/novak-djokovic/matches) owns the longest sequence of Paris Masters match wins: <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">17 consecutive victories</span>, beginning with his title run in [2013](/tournaments/paris-masters/2013) and ending in [2016](/tournaments/paris-masters/2016). [🇩🇪 Boris Becker](/players/boris-becker/matches) follows with <span class="mc-emerald mc-bold">14</span> consecutive wins, while Djokovic recorded another streak of <span class="mc-blue">13</span> from [2019](/tournaments/paris-masters/2019) to [2022](/tournaments/paris-masters/2022). Djokovic also holds the record for consecutive titles, winning <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">3 editions in a row</span> from **2013 to 2015**. → [Full streak leaderboard](/tournaments/paris-masters/records/streak)

## Titles without dropping a set

Djokovic completed the tournament without losing a set <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">twice</span>, in [2014](/tournaments/paris-masters/2014) and [2019](/tournaments/paris-masters/2019). Other straight-sets title campaigns include [🇨🇭 Roger Federer](/players/roger-federer/matches) in [2011](/tournaments/paris-masters/2011), [🇸🇪 Thomas Enqvist](/players/thomas-enqvist/matches) in [1996](/tournaments/paris-masters/1996), and [🇮🇹 Jannik Sinner](/players/jannik-sinner/matches) in [2025](/tournaments/paris-masters/2025). These runs combine a perfect sets record with the ability to handle the demanding indoor draw.

## [Age records](/tournaments/paris-masters/records/ages/main)

The [youngest Paris Masters champion](/tournaments/paris-masters/records/ages/titles) is [🇩🇪 Boris Becker](/players/boris-becker/matches), who won the inaugural [1986](/tournaments/paris-masters/1986) edition at <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">18 years and 339 days</span> at the start of the tournament. [🇩🇰 Holger Rune](/players/holger-rune/matches) follows, winning the [2022](/tournaments/paris-masters/2022) title aged <span class="mc-emerald">19 years and 185 days</span>. The oldest champion is [🇷🇸 Novak Djokovic](/players/novak-djokovic/matches), aged <span class="mc-orange mc-bold">36 years and 161 days</span> at the start of his [2023](/tournaments/paris-masters/2023) title run.

For main-draw participation, [🇫🇷 Fabrice Santoro](/players/fabrice-santoro/matches) was just <span class="mc-gold">16 years old</span> in [1989](/tournaments/paris-masters/1989), while [🇺🇸 Jimmy Connors](/players/jimmy-connors/matches) was already <span class="mc-orange">39</span> in [1991](/tournaments/paris-masters/1991). → [Youngest players by round](/tournaments/paris-masters/records/ages/youngestrounds) · [Oldest players by round](/tournaments/paris-masters/records/ages/oldestrounds)

## [Fewest games lost en route to the title](/tournaments/paris-masters/records/least)

The lowest total of games conceded by a champion in the records table is <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">26</span>, credited to [🇸🇪 Stefan Edberg](/players/stefan-edberg/matches) in [1990](/tournaments/paris-masters/1990), although that edition's final ended with a retirement. [🇷🇺 Nikolay Davydenko](/players/nikolay-davydenko/matches) surrendered just <span class="mc-emerald mc-bold">27 games</span> in [2006](/tournaments/paris-masters/2006), while [🇮🇹 Jannik Sinner](/players/jannik-sinner/matches) lost only <span class="mc-blue mc-bold">29</span> during his [2025](/tournaments/paris-masters/2025) championship run. → [Full least-games-lost leaderboard](/tournaments/paris-masters/records/least)

## [Timespan records](/tournaments/paris-masters/records/timespan)

Djokovic has the longest span between his first and most recent Paris Masters titles: <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">13 years and 359 days</span>, from [2009](/tournaments/paris-masters/2009) to [2023](/tournaments/paris-masters/2023), measured using the tournament dates in the database. Becker's three titles span <span class="mc-emerald">1986–1992</span>, and Safin's <span class="mc-blue">2000–2004</span>. Among champions with two titles, [🇺🇸 Andre Agassi](/players/andre-agassi/matches) waited **five years**, from [1994](/tournaments/paris-masters/1994) to [1999](/tournaments/paris-masters/1999), before winning again. → [Full timespan records](/tournaments/paris-masters/records/timespan)
