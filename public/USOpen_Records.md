## [Winning streaks & Consecutive titles](/tournaments/us-open/records/streak)

[🇨🇭 Roger Federer](/players/roger-federer/matches) holds the record for the longest unbeaten run at the US Open with <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">40 consecutive wins</span> from [2004](/tournaments/us-open/2004) through [2009](/tournaments/us-open/2009). ## Titles without dropping a set

In the TennisMyLife Open Era dataset through the completed 2025 edition, every US Open men's singles champion dropped at least one set on the way to the title. No champion completed the entire title run without conceding a set.

## [Age records](/tournaments/us-open/records/ages/main)

The [youngest champion](/tournaments/us-open/records/ages/titles) in the Open Era is [🇺🇸 Pete Sampras](/players/pete-sampras/matches), who won in [1990](/tournaments/us-open/1990) at <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">19 years and 15 days</span>, defeating [🇺🇸 Andre Agassi](/players/andre-agassi/matches) in the final. The oldest champion is [🇷🇸 Novak Djokovic](/players/novak-djokovic/matches), who won in [2023](/tournaments/us-open/2023) at <span class="mc-orange mc-bold">36 years and 98 days</span>.

The [youngest player to win a match](/tournaments/us-open/records/ages/youngestrounds) is [🇺🇸 Michael Chang](/players/michael-chang/matches), at <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">15 years and 191 days</span> in [1987](/tournaments/us-open/1987). The [oldest match winner](/tournaments/us-open/records/ages/oldestrounds) is [🇪🇨 Pancho Segura](/players/pancho-segura/matches), at <span class="mc-orange mc-bold">49 years and 74 days</span> in [1970](/tournaments/us-open/1970). The youngest participant in the database is [🇺🇸 Tommy Ho](/players/tommy-ho/matches), who appeared at <span class="mc-gold">15 years and 73 days</span> in [1988](/tournaments/us-open/1988).

## [Timespan records](/tournaments/us-open/records/timespan)

[🇷🇸 Novak Djokovic](/players/novak-djokovic/matches) and [🇺🇸 Pete Sampras](/players/pete-sampras/matches) share the record for the longest span between first and last US Open title: <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">12 years</span>. Djokovic won from [2011](/tournaments/us-open/2011) to [2023](/tournaments/us-open/2023), while Sampras won from [1990](/tournaments/us-open/1990) to [2002](/tournaments/us-open/2002). The longest gap between two title wins by the same player is <span class="mc-emerald mc-bold">6 years</span>, achieved by Sampras between [1996](/tournaments/us-open/1996) and [2002](/tournaments/us-open/2002). The Open Era record runs from [1968](/tournaments/us-open/1968), won by [🇺🇸 Arthur Ashe](/players/arthur-ashe/matches), through [2025](/tournaments/us-open/2025), won by [🇪🇸 Carlos Alcaraz](/players/carlos-alcaraz/matches). → [Full timespan records](/tournaments/us-open/records/timespan)
