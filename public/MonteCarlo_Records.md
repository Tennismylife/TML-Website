## [Titles won](/tournaments/monte-carlo-masters/records/count/titles)

The all-time record for titles at Monte Carlo belongs to [🇪🇸 Rafael Nadal](/players/rafael-nadal), who has lifted the trophy <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">11 times</span> — a mark that looks virtually unassailable. Behind him, on <span class="mc-emerald mc-bold">3 titles</span>, stand [🇷🇴 Ilie Nastase](/players/ilie-nastase), [🇸🇪 Bjorn Borg](/players/bjorn-borg), [🇦🇹 Thomas Muster](/players/thomas-muster), and [🇬🇷 Stefanos Tsitsipas](/players/stefanos-tsitsipas). Others to have won more than once: [🇦🇷 Vilas](/players/guillermo-vilas) <span class="mc-blue">(2)</span>, [🇸🇪 Wilander](/players/mats-wilander) <span class="mc-blue">(2)</span>, [🇨🇿 Lendl](/players/ivan-lendl) <span class="mc-blue">(2)</span>, [🇪🇸 Bruguera](/players/sergi-bruguera) <span class="mc-blue">(2)</span>, [🇧🇷 Kuerten](/players/gustavo-kuerten) <span class="mc-blue">(2)</span>.

## [Winning streaks & Consecutive titles](/tournaments/monte-carlo-masters/records/streak)

[🇪🇸 Rafael Nadal](/players/rafael-nadal) holds the record for the longest unbeaten run: <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">46 consecutive wins</span> between [2005](/tournaments/monte-carlo-masters/2005) and [2013](/tournaments/monte-carlo-masters/2013). He also holds the record for consecutive titles: <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">8 in a row</span> from [2005](/tournaments/monte-carlo-masters/2005) to [2012](/tournaments/monte-carlo-masters/2012). Second on the streak list: [🇷🇴 Ilie Nastase](/players/ilie-nastase) (<span class="mc-emerald">19</span>), then [🇸🇪 Borg](/players/bjorn-borg) (<span class="mc-blue">14</span>), [🇪🇸 Ferrero](/players/juan-carlos-ferrero) (<span class="mc-blue">12</span>), [🇬🇷 Tsitsipas](/players/stefanos-tsitsipas) (<span class="mc-blue">12</span>), [🇦🇷 Coria](/players/guillermo-coria) (<span class="mc-blue">11</span>). → [Full streak leaderboard](/tournaments/monte-carlo-masters/records/streak)

## [Age records](/tournaments/monte-carlo-masters/records/ages/main)

The [youngest champion](/tournaments/monte-carlo-masters/records/ages/titles) in tournament history is [🇸🇪 Mats Wilander](/players/mats-wilander), who won the title at just <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">18 years and 217 days</span> in [1983](/tournaments/monte-carlo-masters/1983). The oldest champion is [🇮🇹 Nicola Pietrangeli](/players/nicola-pietrangeli) at <span class="mc-orange mc-bold">34 years and 209 days</span> ([1968](/tournaments/monte-carlo-masters/1968)).

The [youngest player to win a match](/tournaments/monte-carlo-masters/records/ages/youngestrounds) is [🇫🇷 Richard Gasquet](/players/richard-gasquet) at <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">15 years and 301 days</span> ([2002](/tournaments/monte-carlo-masters/2002)), while the [oldest match winner](/tournaments/monte-carlo-masters/records/ages/oldestrounds) is [🇪🇨 Pancho Segura](/players/pancho-segura) at <span class="mc-orange mc-bold">47 years and 298 days</span> ([1969](/tournaments/monte-carlo-masters/1969)). The oldest participant ever is [🇺🇸 Gardnar Mulloy](/players/gardnar-mulloy), who took the court at <span class="mc-orange">55 years and 143 days</span> in [1969](/tournaments/monte-carlo-masters/1969).

## [Timespan records](/tournaments/monte-carlo-masters/records/timespan)

[🇪🇸 Rafael Nadal](/players/rafael-nadal) holds the record for the longest span between first and last title: <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">13 years</span> ([2005](/tournaments/monte-carlo-masters/2005)–[2018](/tournaments/monte-carlo-masters/2018)). The longest gap between two titles belongs to [🇦🇷 Guillermo Vilas](/players/guillermo-vilas) with <span class="mc-emerald mc-bold">6 years</span> ([1976](/tournaments/monte-carlo-masters/1976)–[1982](/tournaments/monte-carlo-masters/1982)). The tournament has been held from [1968](/tournaments/monte-carlo-masters/1968) ([🇮🇹 Pietrangeli](/players/nicola-pietrangeli)) through [2025](/tournaments/monte-carlo-masters/2025) ([🇪🇸 Alcaraz](/players/carlos-alcaraz)). → [Full timespan records](/tournaments/monte-carlo-masters/records/timespan)

## [Ten youngest champions in history](/tournaments/monte-carlo-masters/records/ages/titles)

The list of the youngest champions ever is topped by [🇸🇪 Mats Wilander](/players/mats-wilander), who won in [1983](/tournaments/monte-carlo-masters/1983) aged just <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">18 years and 217 days</span>. Second is [🇪🇸 Rafael Nadal](/players/rafael-nadal) at <span class="mc-blue">18y 312d</span> ([2005](/tournaments/monte-carlo-masters/2005)), followed by [🇺🇦 Andrei Medvedev](/players/andrei-medvedev) (<span class="mc-blue">19y 230d</span>, [1994](/tournaments/monte-carlo-masters/1994)), Nadal again (<span class="mc-blue">19y 318d</span>, [2006](/tournaments/monte-carlo-masters/2006)), and [🇦🇷 Alberto Mancini](/players/alberto-mancini) (<span class="mc-blue">19y 339d</span>, [1989](/tournaments/monte-carlo-masters/1989)).

The rest of the top ten: [🇸🇪 Henrik Sundstrom](/players/henrik-sundstrom) (<span class="mc-blue">20y 47d</span>, [1984](/tournaments/monte-carlo-masters/1984)), [🇪🇸 Sergi Bruguera](/players/sergi-bruguera) (<span class="mc-blue">20y 96d</span>, [1991](/tournaments/monte-carlo-masters/1991)), [🇸🇪 Bjorn Borg](/players/bjorn-borg) (<span class="mc-blue">20y 303d</span>, [1977](/tournaments/monte-carlo-masters/1977)), Nadal once more (<span class="mc-blue">20y 315d</span>, [2007](/tournaments/monte-carlo-masters/2007)), and [🇨🇱 Marcelo Rios](/players/marcelo-rios) (<span class="mc-blue">21y 116d</span>, [1997](/tournaments/monte-carlo-masters/1997)).
