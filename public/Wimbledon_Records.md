## [Winning streaks & Consecutive titles](/tournaments/wimbledon/records/streak)

[🇸🇪 Bjorn Borg](/players/bjorn-borg/matches) holds the record for the longest unbeaten run: <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">41 consecutive wins</span> between [1976](/tournaments/wimbledon/1976) and [1981](/tournaments/wimbledon/1981). He also holds the record for consecutive titles: <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">5 in a row</span> from [1976](/tournaments/wimbledon/1976) to [1980](/tournaments/wimbledon/1980). ## [Age records](/tournaments/wimbledon/records/ages/main)

The [youngest champion](/tournaments/wimbledon/records/ages/titles) in tournament history is [🇩🇪 Boris Becker](/players/boris-becker/matches), who won the title at just <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">17 years and 214 days</span> in [1985](/tournaments/wimbledon/1985), defeating [🇺🇸 Kevin Curren](/players/kevin-curren/matches) in the final. The oldest champion is [🇨🇭 Roger Federer](/players/roger-federer/matches) at <span class="mc-orange mc-bold">35 years and 329 days</span> ([2017](/tournaments/wimbledon/2017)).

The [youngest player to win a match](/tournaments/wimbledon/records/ages/youngestrounds) is [🇺🇸 Michael Chang](/players/michael-chang/matches) at <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">16 years and 119 days</span> ([1988](/tournaments/wimbledon/1988)), while the [oldest match winner](/tournaments/wimbledon/records/ages/oldestrounds) is [🇺🇸 Richard Pancho Gonzales](/players/pancho-gonzales/matches) at <span class="mc-orange mc-bold">44 years and 47 days</span> ([1972](/tournaments/wimbledon/1972)). The youngest participant ever is [🇺🇸 Michael Chang](/players/michael-chang/matches), who took to the court at <span class="mc-gold">16 years and 119 days</span> in [1988](/tournaments/wimbledon/1988), while the oldest participant ever is [🇦🇺 Bob Howe](/players/bob-howe/matches) at <span class="mc-orange">45 years and 321 days</span> in [1971](/tournaments/wimbledon/1971).

## [Timespan records](/tournaments/wimbledon/records/timespan)

[🇨🇭 Roger Federer](/players/roger-federer/matches) holds the record for the longest span between first and last title: <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">14 years</span> ([2003](/tournaments/wimbledon/2003)–[2017](/tournaments/wimbledon/2017)). The longest gap between two title wins by the same player is <span class="mc-emerald mc-bold">8 years</span>, achieved by [🇺🇸 Jimmy Connors](/players/jimmy-connors/matches) between [1974](/tournaments/wimbledon/1974) and [1982](/tournaments/wimbledon/1982). The tournament has been held from [1968](/tournaments/wimbledon/1968) ([🇦🇺 Rod Laver](/players/rod-laver/matches)) through [2025](/tournaments/wimbledon/2025) ([🇮🇹 Jannik Sinner](/players/jannik-sinner/matches)). → [Full timespan records](/tournaments/wimbledon/records/timespan)

## [Ten youngest champions in history](/tournaments/wimbledon/records/ages/titles)

The list of the youngest champions ever is topped by [🇩🇪 Boris Becker](/players/boris-becker/matches), who won in [1985](/tournaments/wimbledon/1985) aged just <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">17 years and 214 days</span>. Second is [🇩🇪 Boris Becker](/players/boris-becker/matches) at <span class="mc-blue">18 years and 213 days</span> ([1986](/tournaments/wimbledon/1986)), followed by [🇸🇪 Bjorn Borg](/players/bjorn-borg/matches) (<span class="mc-blue">20 years and 14 days</span>, [1976](/tournaments/wimbledon/1976)), [🇪🇸 Carlos Alcaraz](/players/carlos-alcaraz/matches) (<span class="mc-blue">20 years and 59 days</span>, [2023](/tournaments/wimbledon/2023)), and [🇸🇪 Bjorn Borg](/players/bjorn-borg/matches) (<span class="mc-blue">21 years and 13 days</span>, [1977](/tournaments/wimbledon/1977)).

The rest of the top ten: [🇪🇸 Carlos Alcaraz](/players/carlos-alcaraz/matches) (<span class="mc-blue">21 years and 57 days</span>, [2024](/tournaments/wimbledon/2024)), [🇦🇺 Lleyton Hewitt](/players/lleyton-hewitt/matches) (<span class="mc-blue">21 years and 119 days</span>, [2002](/tournaments/wimbledon/2002)), [🇩🇪 Boris Becker](/players/boris-becker/matches) (<span class="mc-blue">21 years and 216 days</span>, [1989](/tournaments/wimbledon/1989)), [🇺🇸 Jimmy Connors](/players/jimmy-connors/matches) (<span class="mc-blue">21 years and 294 days</span>, [1974](/tournaments/wimbledon/1974)), and [🇺🇸 Pete Sampras](/players/pete-sampras/matches) (<span class="mc-blue">21 years and 313 days</span>, [1993](/tournaments/wimbledon/1993)).
