## [Titles won](/tournaments/miami-masters/records/count/titles)

[🇺🇸 Andre Agassi](/players/andre-agassi/matches) and [🇷🇸 Novak Djokovic](/players/novak-djokovic/matches) share the all-time Miami title record with <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">6 championships each</span>. Agassi triumphed in [1990](/tournaments/miami-masters/1990), [1995](/tournaments/miami-masters/1995), [1996](/tournaments/miami-masters/1996) and [2001](/tournaments/miami-masters/2001)–[2003](/tournaments/miami-masters/2003); Djokovic won in [2007](/tournaments/miami-masters/2007), [2011](/tournaments/miami-masters/2011), [2012](/tournaments/miami-masters/2012) and [2014](/tournaments/miami-masters/2014)–[2016](/tournaments/miami-masters/2016). [🇨🇭 Roger Federer](/players/roger-federer/matches) ranks third with <span class="mc-emerald mc-bold">4 titles</span>, followed by [🇺🇸 Pete Sampras](/players/pete-sampras/matches) with <span class="mc-blue">3</span>. Two-time champions include [🇮🇹 Jannik Sinner](/players/jannik-sinner/matches), [🇨🇿 Ivan Lendl](/players/ivan-lendl/matches), [🇺🇸 Andy Roddick](/players/andy-roddick/matches) and [🇬🇧 Andy Murray](/players/andy-murray/matches). Sinner most recently won in [2026](/tournaments/miami-masters/2026), defeating [🇨🇿 Jiri Lehecka](/players/jiri-lehecka/matches). → [Full titles leaderboard](/tournaments/miami-masters/records/count/titles)

## Titles without dropping a set

[🇷🇸 Novak Djokovic](/players/novak-djokovic/matches) won Miami without losing a set in <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">three editions</span>: [2007](/tournaments/miami-masters/2007), [2012](/tournaments/miami-masters/2012) and [2016](/tournaments/miami-masters/2016), according to the recorded match scores. [🇮🇹 Jannik Sinner](/players/jannik-sinner/matches) joined the list in [2026](/tournaments/miami-masters/2026), winning all six matches in straight sets and defeating [🇨🇿 Jiri Lehecka](/players/jiri-lehecka/matches) <span class="mc-emerald">6–4, 6–4</span> in the final.
