## [Titles won](/tournaments/madrid-masters/records/count/titles)

The all-time record for titles at Madrid belongs to [🇪🇸 Rafael Nadal](/players/rafael-nadal/matches), who has lifted the trophy <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">5 times</span> — a mark that looks virtually unassailable. Behind him, on <span class="mc-emerald mc-bold">3 titles</span>, stand [🇨🇭 Roger Federer](/players/roger-federer/matches) and [🇷🇸 Novak Djokovic](/players/novak-djokovic/matches). Others to have won the tournament more than once: [🇪🇸 Carlos Alcaraz](/players/carlos-alcaraz/matches) <span class="mc-blue">(2)</span>, [🇩🇪 Alexander Zverev](/players/alexander-zverev/matches) <span class="mc-blue">(2)</span>, [🇬🇧 Andy Murray](/players/andy-murray/matches) <span class="mc-blue">(2)</span>.

## [Age records](/tournaments/madrid-masters/records/ages/main)

The [youngest champion](/tournaments/madrid-masters/records/ages/titles) in tournament history is [🇪🇸 Carlos Alcaraz](/players/carlos-alcaraz/matches), who won the title at just <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">18 years and 362 days</span> in [2022](/tournaments/madrid-masters/2022). The oldest champion is [🇺🇸 Andre Agassi](/players/andre-agassi/matches) at <span class="mc-orange mc-bold">32 years and 168 days</span> ([2002](/tournaments/madrid-masters/2002)).

The [youngest player to win a match](/tournaments/madrid-masters/records/ages/youngestrounds) is [🇵🇹 João Fonseca](/players/joao-fonseca/matches) at <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">17 years and 244 days</span> ([2024](/tournaments/madrid-masters/2024)), while the [oldest match winner](/tournaments/madrid-masters/records/ages/oldestrounds) is [🇫🇷 Gaël Monfils](/players/gael-monfils/matches) at <span class="mc-orange mc-bold">38 years and 232 days</span> ([2025](/tournaments/madrid-masters/2025)). The oldest participant ever is [🇩🇪 Tommy Haas](/players/tommy-haas/matches), who took the court at <span class="mc-orange">39 years and 35 days</span> in [2017](/tournaments/madrid-masters/2017).

## [Timespan records](/tournaments/madrid-masters/records/timespan)

[🇪🇸 Rafael Nadal](/players/rafael-nadal/matches) holds the record for the longest span between first and last title: <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">12 years</span> ([2005](/tournaments/madrid-masters/2005)–[2017](/tournaments/madrid-masters/2017)). The longest gap between two titles belongs to [🇬🇧 Andy Murray](/players/andy-murray/matches) with <span class="mc-emerald mc-bold">7 years</span> ([2008](/tournaments/madrid-masters/2008)–[2015](/tournaments/madrid-masters/2015)). The tournament has been held from [2002](/tournaments/madrid-masters/2002) ([🇺🇸 Andre Agassi](/players/andre-agassi/matches)) through [2025](/tournaments/madrid-masters/2025) ([🇳🇴 Casper Ruud](/players/casper-ruud/matches)). → [Full timespan records](/tournaments/madrid-masters/records/timespan)
