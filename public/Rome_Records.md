## [Winning streaks & Consecutive titles](/tournaments/rome-masters/records/streak)

## [Age records](/tournaments/rome-masters/records/ages/main)

The [youngest champion](/tournaments/rome-masters/records/ages/titles) in tournament history is [🇸🇪 Bjorn Borg](/players/bjorn-borg/matches), who won the title at just <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">17 years and 352 days</span> in [1974](/tournaments/rome-masters/1974). The oldest champion is [🇷🇸 Novak Djokovic](/players/novak-djokovic/matches) at <span class="mc-orange mc-bold">34 years and 352 days</span> ([2022](/tournaments/rome-masters/2022)).

The [youngest player to win a match](/tournaments/rome-masters/records/ages/youngestrounds) is [🇸🇪 Kent Carlsson](/players/kent-carlsson/matches) at <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">16 years and 131 days</span> ([1984](/tournaments/rome-masters/1984)), while the [oldest match winner](/tournaments/rome-masters/records/ages/oldestrounds) is [🇮🇹 Giuseppe Merlo](/players/giuseppe-merlo/matches) at <span class="mc-orange mc-bold">45 years and 234 days</span> ([1973](/tournaments/rome-masters/1973)). The oldest participant ever is [🇺🇸 Aaron Krickstein](/players/aaron-krickstein/matches), who took the court at <span class="mc-orange">15 years and 287 days</span> in [1983](/tournaments/rome-masters/1983).

## [Timespan records](/tournaments/rome-masters/records/timespan)

[🇪🇸 Rafael Nadal](/players/rafael-nadal/matches) holds the record for the longest span between first and last title: <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">16 years</span> ([2005](/tournaments/rome-masters/2005)–[2021](/tournaments/rome-masters/2021)). The longest gap between two appearances belongs to [🇩🇪 Tommy Haas](/players/tommy-haas/matches) from 1997-05-12 to 2017-05-15, <span class="mc-emerald mc-bold">20 years and 8 days</span>. The tournament has been held from [1968](/tournaments/rome-masters/1968) ([🇳🇱 Tom Okker](/players/tom-okker/matches)) through [2025](/tournaments/rome-masters/2025) ([🇪🇸 Carlos Alcaraz](/players/carlos-alcaraz/matches)). → [Full timespan records](/tournaments/rome-masters/records/timespan)

## [Ten youngest champions in history](/tournaments/rome-masters/records/ages/titles)

The list of the youngest champions ever is topped by [🇸🇪 Bjorn Borg](/players/bjorn-borg/matches), who won in [1974](/tournaments/rome-masters/1974) aged just <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">17 years and 352 days</span>. Second is [🇺🇸 Jimmy Arias](/players/jimmy-arias/matches) at <span class="mc-blue">18 years and 272 days</span> ([1983](/tournaments/rome-masters/1983)), followed by [🇪🇸 Rafael Nadal](/players/rafael-nadal/matches) (<span class="mc-blue">18 years and 333 days</span>, [2005](/tournaments/rome-masters/2005)), [🇦🇷 Alberto Mancini](/players/alberto-mancini/matches) (<span class="mc-blue">19 years and 360 days</span>, [1989](/tournaments/rome-masters/1989)), and [🇩🇪 Alexander Zverev](/players/alexander-zverev/matches) (<span class="mc-blue">20 years and 24 days</span>, [2017](/tournaments/rome-masters/2017)).

The rest of the top ten: [🇪🇸 Rafael Nadal](/players/rafael-nadal/matches) (<span class="mc-blue">20 years and 337 days</span>, [2007](/tournaments/rome-masters/2007)), [🇷🇸 Novak Djokovic](/players/novak-djokovic/matches) (<span class="mc-blue">20 years and 349 days</span>, [2008](/tournaments/rome-masters/2008)), [🇪🇸 Juan Carlos Ferrero](/players/juan-carlos-ferrero/matches) (<span class="mc-blue">21 years and 84 days</span>, [2001](/tournaments/rome-masters/2001)), and [🇺🇸 Jim Courier](/players/jim-courier/matches) (<span class="mc-blue">21 years and 267 days</span>, [1992](/tournaments/rome-masters/1992)).
