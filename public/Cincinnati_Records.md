## [Winning streaks & Consecutive titles](/tournaments/cincinnati-masters/records/streak)

[🇸🇪 Mats Wilander](/players/mats-wilander/matches) holds the record for the longest unbeaten run: <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">17 consecutive wins</span> between [1983](/tournaments/cincinnati-masters/1983) and [1985](/tournaments/cincinnati-masters/1985). [🇺🇸 Michael Chang](/players/michael-chang/matches) and [🇨🇭 Roger Federer](/players/roger-federer/matches) share second place with <span class="mc-emerald">14</span>, followed by [🇺🇸 Cliff Richey](/players/cliff-richey/matches) (<span class="mc-blue">12</span>) and [🇩🇪 Boris Becker](/players/boris-becker/matches) and [🇸🇪 Stefan Edberg](/players/stefan-edberg/matches) (<span class="mc-blue">11 each</span>). The record for consecutive titles is <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">2 in a row</span>, achieved by Wilander, Chang, [🇺🇸 Andre Agassi](/players/andre-agassi/matches), and Federer. → [Full streak leaderboard](/tournaments/cincinnati-masters/records/streak)

## Titles without dropping a set

[🇨🇭 Roger Federer](/players/roger-federer/matches) and [🇺🇸 Pete Sampras](/players/pete-sampras/matches) share the record, each winning the title without conceding a set <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">2 times</span>. Federer did so in [2012](/tournaments/cincinnati-masters/2012) and [2015](/tournaments/cincinnati-masters/2015), while Sampras achieved the feat in [1997](/tournaments/cincinnati-masters/1997) and [1999](/tournaments/cincinnati-masters/1999). Others: [🇺🇸 Stan Smith](/players/stan-smith/matches) <span class="mc-emerald">(1×)</span>, [🇺🇸 Marty Riessen](/players/marty-riessen/matches) <span class="mc-emerald">(1×)</span>, [🇺🇸 Harold Solomon](/players/harold-solomon/matches) <span class="mc-blue">(1×)</span>, [🇨🇿 Ivan Lendl](/players/ivan-lendl/matches) <span class="mc-blue">(1×)</span>, [🇸🇪 Mats Wilander](/players/mats-wilander/matches) <span class="mc-blue">(1×)</span>, [🇸🇪 Stefan Edberg](/players/stefan-edberg/matches) <span class="mc-blue">(1×)</span>, [🇪🇸 Carlos Moya](/players/carlos-moya/matches) <span class="mc-blue">(1×)</span>, [🇬🇧 Andy Murray](/players/andy-murray/matches) <span class="mc-blue">(1×)</span>, and [🇧🇬 Grigor Dimitrov](/players/grigor-dimitrov/matches) <span class="mc-blue">(1×)</span>.

## [Age records](/tournaments/cincinnati-masters/records/ages/main)

The [youngest champion](/tournaments/cincinnati-masters/records/ages/titles) in tournament history is [🇩🇪 Boris Becker](/players/boris-becker/matches), who won the title at just <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">17 years and 271 days</span> in [1985](/tournaments/cincinnati-masters/1985). The oldest champion is [🇷🇸 Novak Djokovic](/players/novak-djokovic/matches) at <span class="mc-orange mc-bold">36 years and 84 days</span> ([2023](/tournaments/cincinnati-masters/2023)).

The [youngest player to win a match](/tournaments/cincinnati-masters/records/ages/youngestrounds) is [🇺🇸 Michael Chang](/players/michael-chang/matches) at <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">16 years and 175 days</span> ([1988](/tournaments/cincinnati-masters/1988)), while the [oldest match winner](/tournaments/cincinnati-masters/records/ages/oldestrounds) is [🇩🇰 Torben Ulrich](/players/torben-ulrich/matches) at <span class="mc-orange mc-bold">44 years and 306 days</span> ([1973](/tournaments/cincinnati-masters/1973)). The oldest participant ever is [🇺🇸 Al Bunis](/players/al-bunis/matches), who took the court at <span class="mc-orange">45 years and 162 days</span> in [1969](/tournaments/cincinnati-masters/1969).

## [Timespan records](/tournaments/cincinnati-masters/records/timespan)

[🇨🇭 Roger Federer](/players/roger-federer/matches) holds the record for the longest span between first and last title: <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">10 years</span> ([2005](/tournaments/cincinnati-masters/2005)–[2015](/tournaments/cincinnati-masters/2015)). The longest gap between two titles belongs to [🇺🇸 Andre Agassi](/players/andre-agassi/matches), with <span class="mc-emerald mc-bold">8 years</span> between [1996](/tournaments/cincinnati-masters/1996) and [2004](/tournaments/cincinnati-masters/2004). The completed Open Era record in the database spans from [1969](/tournaments/cincinnati-masters/1969) ([🇺🇸 Cliff Richey](/players/cliff-richey/matches)) through [2025](/tournaments/cincinnati-masters/2025) ([🇪🇸 Carlos Alcaraz](/players/carlos-alcaraz/matches)). → [Full timespan records](/tournaments/cincinnati-masters/records/timespan)
