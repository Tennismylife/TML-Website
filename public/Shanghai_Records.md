## [Titles won](/tournaments/shanghai/records/count/titles)

The all-time record for titles at Shanghai belongs to [🇷🇸 Novak Djokovic](/players/novak-djokovic/matches), who has lifted the trophy <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">4 times</span>. [🇬🇧 Andy Murray](/players/andy-murray/matches) follows with <span class="mc-emerald mc-bold">3 titles</span>, while [🇨🇭 Roger Federer](/players/roger-federer/matches) won it <span class="mc-blue mc-bold">2 times</span>. The other champions in the database are [🇷🇺 Nikolay Davydenko](/players/nikolay-davydenko/matches), [🇷🇺 Daniil Medvedev](/players/daniil-medvedev/matches), [🇵🇱 Hubert Hurkacz](/players/hubert-hurkacz/matches), [🇮🇹 Jannik Sinner](/players/jannik-sinner/matches), and [🇲🇨 Valentin Vacherot](/players/valentin-vacherot/matches), with one title each.

## [Winning streaks & Consecutive titles](/tournaments/shanghai/records/streak)

[🇷🇸 Novak Djokovic](/players/novak-djokovic/matches) owns the longest winning streak in Shanghai history: <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">13 consecutive wins</span> from [2012](/tournaments/shanghai/2012) through [2014](/tournaments/shanghai/2014). [🇬🇧 Andy Murray](/players/andy-murray/matches) is next with <span class="mc-emerald mc-bold">12</span> from [2010](/tournaments/shanghai/2010) through [2012](/tournaments/shanghai/2012), followed by [🇨🇭 Roger Federer](/players/roger-federer/matches) with <span class="mc-blue">8</span>. ## Titles without dropping a set

[🇬🇧 Andy Murray](/players/andy-murray/matches) and [🇷🇸 Novak Djokovic](/players/novak-djokovic/matches) share the record, each winning the Shanghai title without dropping a set <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">2 times</span>. Murray did it in [2010](/tournaments/shanghai/2010) and [2016](/tournaments/shanghai/2016), while Djokovic achieved the feat in [2015](/tournaments/shanghai/2015) and [2018](/tournaments/shanghai/2018). [🇷🇺 Daniil Medvedev](/players/daniil-medvedev/matches) also completed a perfect straight-sets title run in [2019](/tournaments/shanghai/2019).

## [Age records](/tournaments/shanghai/records/ages/main)

The [youngest champion](/tournaments/shanghai/records/ages/titles) in Shanghai history is [🇮🇹 Jannik Sinner](/players/jannik-sinner/matches), who won the [2024](/tournaments/shanghai/2024) title at <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">23 years and 47 days</span>. The oldest champion is [🇨🇭 Roger Federer](/players/roger-federer/matches), aged <span class="mc-orange mc-bold">36 years and 62 days</span> when he won in [2017](/tournaments/shanghai/2017).

The youngest main-draw participant recorded in the database is [🇨🇳 Yibing Wu](/players/yibing-wu/matches) at <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">17 years and 361 days</span> in [2017](/tournaments/shanghai/2017), while the oldest is [🇨🇭 Stan Wawrinka](/players/stan-wawrinka/matches) at <span class="mc-orange mc-bold">40 years and 180 days</span> in [2025](/tournaments/shanghai/2025).

## [Timespan records](/tournaments/shanghai/records/timespan)

[🇷🇸 Novak Djokovic](/players/novak-djokovic/matches) and [🇬🇧 Andy Murray](/players/andy-murray/matches) share the longest span between first and last Shanghai titles at <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">6 years and 2 days</span>: Djokovic from [2012](/tournaments/shanghai/2012) to [2018](/tournaments/shanghai/2018), and Murray from [2010](/tournaments/shanghai/2010) to [2016](/tournaments/shanghai/2016). Murray also owns the longest gap between two titles, <span class="mc-emerald mc-bold">5 years</span> between [2011](/tournaments/shanghai/2011) and [2016](/tournaments/shanghai/2016). The completed tournament record in the database spans from [2009](/tournaments/shanghai/2009), won by [🇷🇺 Nikolay Davydenko](/players/nikolay-davydenko/matches), through [2025](/tournaments/shanghai/2025), won by [🇲🇨 Valentin Vacherot](/players/valentin-vacherot/matches). → [Full timespan records](/tournaments/shanghai/records/timespan)
