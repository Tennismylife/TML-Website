## [Titles won](/tournaments/atp-finals/records/count/titles)

The all-time ATP Finals singles title record belongs to [🇷🇸 Novak Djokovic](/players/novak-djokovic/matches), with <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">7 titles</span> ([2008](/tournaments/atp-finals/2008), [2012](/tournaments/atp-finals/2012)–[2015](/tournaments/atp-finals/2015), [2022](/tournaments/atp-finals/2022), [2023](/tournaments/atp-finals/2023)). [🇨🇭 Roger Federer](/players/roger-federer/matches) follows with <span class="mc-emerald mc-bold">6</span>; [🇺🇸 Pete Sampras](/players/pete-sampras/matches) and [🇨🇿 Ivan Lendl](/players/ivan-lendl/matches) won <span class="mc-blue">5 each</span>, while [🇷🇴 Ilie Nastase](/players/ilie-nastase/matches) won <span class="mc-blue">4</span>. Three-time champions include [🇺🇸 John McEnroe](/players/john-mcenroe/matches) and [🇩🇪 Boris Becker](/players/boris-becker/matches). Two-time champions include [🇸🇪 Bjorn Borg](/players/bjorn-borg/matches), [🇦🇺 Lleyton Hewitt](/players/lleyton-hewitt/matches), [🇩🇪 Alexander Zverev](/players/alexander-zverev/matches), and [🇮🇹 Jannik Sinner](/players/jannik-sinner/matches). → [Full titles leaderboard](/tournaments/atp-finals/records/count/titles)

## [Winning streaks & Consecutive titles](/tournaments/atp-finals/records/streak)

[🇨🇿 Ivan Lendl](/players/ivan-lendl/matches) holds the longest ATP Finals winning streak at <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">16 consecutive matches</span>, followed by [🇷🇸 Novak Djokovic](/players/novak-djokovic/matches) (<span class="mc-emerald mc-bold">15</span>) and [🇨🇭 Roger Federer](/players/roger-federer/matches) (<span class="mc-blue">14</span>). [🇮🇹 Jannik Sinner](/players/jannik-sinner/matches) put together <span class="mc-blue">10 straight wins</span> across his [2024](/tournaments/atp-finals/2024) and [2025](/tournaments/atp-finals/2025) title runs. Djokovic holds the record for consecutive titles, with <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">4 in a row</span> from [2012](/tournaments/atp-finals/2012) to [2015](/tournaments/atp-finals/2015); Lendl and [🇷🇴 Ilie Nastase](/players/ilie-nastase/matches) each achieved three straight titles. → [Full streak leaderboard](/tournaments/atp-finals/records/streak)

## Undefeated championship campaigns

The ATP Finals round-robin format makes a perfect championship particularly noteworthy: a player can lose a group match and still win the title. [🇮🇹 Jannik Sinner](/players/jannik-sinner/matches) finished both [2024](/tournaments/atp-finals/2024) and [2025](/tournaments/atp-finals/2025) with <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">5 wins, no defeats, and no sets lost</span> in either edition. [🇧🇬 Grigor Dimitrov](/players/grigor-dimitrov/matches) also completed an unbeaten <span class="mc-emerald">5–0</span> title campaign in [2017](/tournaments/atp-finals/2017), although his victories were not all in straight sets. These are records of complete tournament campaigns, including the group stage and knockout rounds.

## [Age records](/tournaments/atp-finals/records/ages/main)

The [youngest ATP Finals champion](/tournaments/atp-finals/records/ages/titles) is [🇺🇸 John McEnroe](/players/john-mcenroe/matches), whose age at the start of the <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">1978 edition was 19 years and 328 days</span>; that edition was played in January 1979. [🇷🇸 Novak Djokovic](/players/novak-djokovic/matches) is the oldest champion, listed at <span class="mc-orange mc-bold">36 years and 175 days</span> at the start of the [2023](/tournaments/atp-finals/2023) edition.

Among recorded participants, [🇺🇸 Aaron Krickstein](/players/aaron-krickstein/matches) was the youngest at <span class="mc-gold">17 years and approximately 160 days</span> in [1984](/tournaments/atp-finals/1984), while [🇨🇭 Roger Federer](/players/roger-federer/matches) competed at <span class="mc-orange">38 years and approximately 95 days</span> in [2019](/tournaments/atp-finals/2019). → [Youngest champions](/tournaments/atp-finals/records/ages/titles) · [Age records by round](/tournaments/atp-finals/records/ages/youngestrounds)

## [Fewest games lost & Tournament formats](/tournaments/atp-finals/records/least)

Unlike a Grand Slam, the ATP Finals has not always had the same draw structure. Most editions have featured round-robin play followed by elimination rounds, while the [1982](/tournaments/atp-finals/1982)–[1985](/tournaments/atp-finals/1985) editions used knockout draws. Consequently, totals for games conceded across tournament runs are <span class="mc-orange mc-bold">not directly comparable</span> unless the number of matches and the competition format are taken into account. → [Fewest games lost records](/tournaments/atp-finals/records/least) · [Round-by-round records](/tournaments/atp-finals/records/rounds)

## [Timespan records](/tournaments/atp-finals/records/timespan)

[🇷🇸 Novak Djokovic](/players/novak-djokovic/matches) holds the record for the longest span between first and last ATP Finals title: <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">15 years</span>, from [2008](/tournaments/atp-finals/2008) to [2023](/tournaments/atp-finals/2023). [🇨🇭 Roger Federer](/players/roger-federer/matches) ([2003](/tournaments/atp-finals/2003)–[2011](/tournaments/atp-finals/2011)) and [🇺🇸 Pete Sampras](/players/pete-sampras/matches) ([1991](/tournaments/atp-finals/1991)–[1999](/tournaments/atp-finals/1999)) each span eight calendar years. Djokovic also returned to the title after a <span class="mc-emerald mc-bold">seven-year gap</span> between [2015](/tournaments/atp-finals/2015) and [2022](/tournaments/atp-finals/2022). The event's singles champions run from [🇺🇸 Stan Smith](/players/stan-smith/matches) in [1970](/tournaments/atp-finals/1970) to [🇮🇹 Jannik Sinner](/players/jannik-sinner/matches) in [2025](/tournaments/atp-finals/2025). → [Full timespan records](/tournaments/atp-finals/records/timespan)
