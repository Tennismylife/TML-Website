## [Winning streaks & Consecutive titles](/tournaments/canada-masters/records/streak)

[🇨🇿 Ivan Lendl](/players/ivan-lendl/matches) holds the record for the longest unbeaten run: <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">18 consecutive wins</span> between [1987](/tournaments/canada-masters/1987) and [1991](/tournaments/canada-masters/1991). He also holds the record for consecutive titles, winning <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">3 in a row</span> from [1987](/tournaments/canada-masters/1987) to [1989](/tournaments/canada-masters/1989). Second on the streak list is [🇨🇭 Roger Federer](/players/roger-federer/matches) (<span class="mc-emerald">16</span>), followed by [🇺🇸 Andre Agassi](/players/andre-agassi/matches), [🇦🇷 Guillermo Vilas](/players/guillermo-vilas/matches), and [🇷🇸 Novak Djokovic](/players/novak-djokovic/matches) (<span class="mc-blue">13 each</span>). Defending champion [🇺🇸 Ben Shelton](/players/ben-shelton/matches) is next with an active streak of <span class="mc-blue">12</span>. → [Full streak leaderboard](/tournaments/canada-masters/records/streak)

## [Age records](/tournaments/canada-masters/records/ages/main)

The [youngest champion](/tournaments/canada-masters/records/ages/titles) in tournament history is [🇺🇸 Michael Chang](/players/michael-chang/matches), who won the title at just <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">18 years and 152 days</span> in [1990](/tournaments/canada-masters/1990). The oldest champion is [🇪🇸 Rafael Nadal](/players/rafael-nadal/matches) at <span class="mc-orange mc-bold">33 years and 63 days</span> ([2019](/tournaments/canada-masters/2019)).

The [youngest player to win a match](/tournaments/canada-masters/records/ages/youngestrounds) is [🇸🇪 Bjorn Borg](/players/bjorn-borg/matches) at <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">17 years and 75 days</span> ([1973](/tournaments/canada-masters/1973)), while the [oldest match winner](/tournaments/canada-masters/records/ages/oldestrounds) is [🇺🇸 Richard Pancho Gonzales](/players/richard-pancho-gonzales/matches) at <span class="mc-orange mc-bold">42 years and 92 days</span> ([1970](/tournaments/canada-masters/1970)). The oldest participant ever is [🇩🇰 Torben Ulrich](/players/torben-ulrich/matches), who took the court at <span class="mc-orange">45 years and 312 days</span> in [1974](/tournaments/canada-masters/1974).

## [Timespan records](/tournaments/canada-masters/records/timespan)

[🇪🇸 Rafael Nadal](/players/rafael-nadal/matches) holds the record for the longest span between first and last title: <span class="mc-gold mc-bold">14 years</span> ([2005](/tournaments/canada-masters/2005)–[2019](/tournaments/canada-masters/2019)). The longest gap between two titles is <span class="mc-emerald mc-bold">5 years</span>, shared by Nadal ([2008](/tournaments/canada-masters/2008)–[2013](/tournaments/canada-masters/2013) and [2013](/tournaments/canada-masters/2013)–[2018](/tournaments/canada-masters/2018)) and [🇬🇧 Andy Murray](/players/andy-murray/matches) ([2010](/tournaments/canada-masters/2010)–[2015](/tournaments/canada-masters/2015)). The tournament record spans from [1968](/tournaments/canada-masters/1968) ([🇮🇳 Ramanathan Krishnan](/players/ramanathan-krishnan/matches)) through [2026](/tournaments/canada-masters/2026) ([🇺🇸 Ben Shelton](/players/ben-shelton/matches)). → [Full timespan records](/tournaments/canada-masters/records/timespan)
